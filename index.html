Common concerns

Myths vs. reality

MYTH
Short-lets destroy properties.
REALITY
Long-lets hide problems. Short-lets expose them fast. Short-let homes are checked after each guest. So instead of discovering issues months later… you catch them immediately, fix them quickly, and protect the long-term value of the property.
MYTH
I'll be dealing with constant guest issues.
REALITY
We handle everything 24/7. Check-ins, questions, complaints, emergencies — all managed by us. You receive monthly reports and payouts. That's your involvement.
MYTH
Short-let income is unpredictable.
REALITY
We optimize pricing and occupancy year-round. Malta's tourism demand is consistent across most months. Combined with strategic booking management, your income stays stable while capturing seasonal peaks.
MYTH
Management fees eat all the profit.
REALITY
Even with our commission, you earn more than long-term rent. Malta's peak season rates are 2-3x standard monthly rent. We're incentivized to capture that upside for you.
